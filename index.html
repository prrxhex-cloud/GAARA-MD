💻 ARCHITECTURE

Enterprise WhatsApp automation, built for reliability.

GAARA X MD is powered by Void Baileys, the highest-performance multi-device WhatsApp protocol engine. Designed from the ground up for zero-downtime hosting on modern cloud infrastructure like Render, Cloudflare Workers, and Vercel.

Every inbound message passes through our multi-stage Anti-Bug sanitization layer, ReDoS detection algorithms, and memory safety checks before dispatching to our modular command handlers.
